# M59: Every driver refuses a design whose inner splits are not rsplits, do not share the outer fold's frame or its analysis set, or index rows the outer fold holds out

**Status:** done (2026-09-04, PR #69 https://github.com/tidymodels/nestedtune/pull/69)

**Goal:** The five orchestrators refuse, at the call the user wrote and before any fold runs, a nested design whose inner splits are not `rsplit`s, whose inner splits do not all carry one frame that is the outer split's own frame or that split's analysis set, or whose whole-frame inner splits index a row the outer split holds out, naming every offending position.

**Outcome:** `check_inner_splits()` (`R/checks.R`), called last in `check_nested()` after the D-047 rules, raises `nestedtune_bad_design` under the driver's call with three rules: every element of a fold's inner `splits` is an `rsplit`; a fold's inner splits all carry one frame `identical()` to the outer split's `$data` (a pointer fast path, the `nested_resamples()` shape) or to `rsample::analysis()` of that split (the `nested_cv()` shape, deep-compared once per distinct frame, and skipped when the outer split's indices cannot build an analysis set, which is left to `last_fit()`); a whole-frame inner split's `in_id` and non-`NA` `out_id` lie inside the outer `in_id`. A disagreeing fold names every split with what it carries; no frame reaches a message. `malformed_designs()` (`helper-orchestration.R`) carries 83 plantings (18 non-rsplit forms, 18 frame including 3 mixed-frame records, 18 index, 29 from M55), each with the fragments its refusal must contain; the five driver checks files assert class, call and every position, plus `nested_cv()` controls on a data.frame, a tibble and a repeated-row `manual_rset()` outer, and two serial completion runs. `break_fold()` and `break_inner_split()` were re-vehicled inside the design's frame (emptied inner `in_id`s; an appended out-of-range outer index), the three foreign-variable note assertions now reading tune's zero-dimension and past-the-end notes. `@param resamples`, four generated pages, a NEWS entry and the `is_fold_payload()` comment (stand-in payloads and defence in depth) state the rules.

**Decisions:** D-049 supersedes D-047's consequence clause. Plan-gate choices, milestone-local: the index containment rule in scope; `identical()` over a shape check; rewrite the two fixture helpers rather than 43 call sites; keep the parallel fat path as the lean gate.
