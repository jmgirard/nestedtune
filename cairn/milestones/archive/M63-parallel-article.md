# M63: A site-only article runs the outer loop on mirai daemons and shows the result identical to the serial run

**Status:** done (2026-09-05, PR #73 https://github.com/tidymodels/nestedtune/pull/73)

**Goal:** Ship `vignettes/articles/parallel.Rmd` ("Running the outer loop in parallel"), built by pkgdown alone, which starts two mirai daemons, runs the getting-started guide's loop on them, shows the metrics and selections identical to the serial run, and explains the pre-flight, what crosses the wire, the `load_all()` caveat, interrupting and cancelling, and the dispatcher warning.

**Outcome:** `vignettes/articles/parallel.Rmd` guards on `mirai` and `ranger` (one notice with singular and plural branches, then `knit_exit()`), starts `mirai::daemons(2)` printing `status()$connections`, runs the guide's `nested_tune_grid()` on the pool, stops it, reruns serially under the same seed and shows `identical()` on `.metrics`, `.selected` and `.tuning_seed` returning `TRUE` with the value read inline; a second chunk starts `daemons(2, dispatcher = FALSE)`, catches the `nestedtune_pool_not_cancellable` warning under a class-keyed `withCallingHandlers()`, prints its class and its message through `cli::ansi_strip()`, and stops the pool. Prose sections cover the pre-flight (`needed_pkgs()` naming parsnip, ranger and workflows), the wire (split, inner resamples and seeds per fold), the `load_all()` caveat and interrupts, as cross-references to `?nested_tune_grid`. `_pkgdown.yml` gains an `Articles` section below `Guides`, both with navbar headings; NEWS entry. The tarball lists no path under `vignettes/articles/`; two masked builds print the notice alone; the PR's pkgdown job installed mirai 2.7.2 and built the article in 32 s; the published page after the merge holds the executed connections `2` then `0`, the identity `TRUE` and the warning class.
